Efficient Frontier Analysis

Efficient Frontier Analysis (EFA) is generally used to compare options across competing objectives and identify choices for which no other option performs at least as well on every assessed dimension and better on at least one. Here, it compares Price and Quality scores to highlight efficient trade-offs and options that may merit further consideration. EFA is not part of the BCA PQM framework.
